Welcome

This is a dictionary of San Martín Peras Mixtec (Tu'un Savi), an Indigenous language spoken in San Martín Peras, Oaxaca, Mexico. Each entry gives the headword with tone marking, its IPA transcription, English and Spanish glosses, and, where available, a recording of a native speaker.

Search for a word in Mixtec, English, or Spanish, or browse by meaning.

Browse all entries » Browse by semantic domain »

How to use the dictionary

  • Click the play button next to a word to hear it.
  • Click anywhere else on a row to open the full entry (IPA, glosses, speaker, notes).
  • On the Browse page you can filter by speaker and sort alphabetically.
